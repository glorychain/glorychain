# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

glorychain is a project about verifiable truth and accountability. We hold ourselves to the same standard: direct, honest, and respectful interaction.

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and honest
- Be constructive in feedback — critique ideas, not people
- Assume good faith
- Respect that people have different levels of experience
- Accept that disagreements happen — resolve them professionally

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by opening a private issue or emailing the maintainers directly.

Reports will be reviewed and responded to. We will protect the confidentiality of reporters.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on severity and history.
